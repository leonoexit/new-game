# One Good Day — art direction v0.3

## Approved direction

The current visual references are:

- `art/approved/style-references/farm-high-key-style-reference-v0.1.png` for environment color, edge treatment, value grouping, and broad pixel language.
- `art/approved/style-references/mira-tea-interaction-style-reference-v0.1.png` for close character interactions, restrained facial readability, focal clarity, and approximate character proportions.
- `art/approved/characters/mira-character-reference-v0.1.png` for Mira's canonical identity, outfit, proportions, and expression construction.
- `art/approved/locations/iris-forest-edge-location-reference-v0.1.png` for shaded-environment color behavior and the canonical anchors of `iris-forest-edge`.
- `art/approved/event-cards/mira-festival-repairs-v0.1.png` as the first approved event-card illustration reference for the provisional `320x200` logical slot.

Use native pixel construction with readable silhouettes, deliberate hard-edged clusters, colored outlines, and roughly three clear value steps per material. The world should feel fresh, warm, and inviting rather than retro, vintage, sepia, or brown-washed.

## Color behavior

- Keep the overall image high-key and bright.
- Use light yellow-green for open foliage and compact cool-teal shadows.
- Separate important objects with small warm-red, clear-blue, cream, and amber accents.
- Avoid pure-black outlines, muddy midtones, cinematic grading, and texture covering every surface.

## Composition rules

- Camera, crop, and aspect ratio follow the emotional event and its intended UI slot; there is no mandatory top-down view or portrait ratio.
- Prefer one action, one focal relationship, and only a few meaningful props.
- Keep characters and environment on one coherent pixel scale.
- No text, logos, UI, speech bubbles, painterly gradients, or photographic texture.

## Current status

The approved farm image locks the color behavior, edge treatment, value grouping, and broad pixel language. The approved Mira tea interaction validates the direction at close character distance and establishes restrained faces and roughly four-head proportions as the current working baseline.

Mira's v0.1 character sheet is now canonical. Her chestnut bob, berry-red hair accessory, cream blouse, honey apron, brown skirt and boots, compact proportions, and approved facial construction must be preserved in identity-sensitive art. Camera angle, scene ratio, protagonist design, and the quantity and design of future characters remain unlocked.

The approved Iris forest-edge reference proves that the same direction remains legible in a shaded, wet environment with a lower camera and a different material mix. The direction-lock milestone now has an approved environment overview, close character interaction, canonical character sheet, and second environment state.

The first event-card illustration and a minimal test screen now exist. User visual testing of `prototype/event-card/index.html` is required before the slot or asset becomes `runtime approved`.
