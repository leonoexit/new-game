# Spring Farming v1 — hands-on milestone checklist

This is the single manual checklist for the milestone. Automated tests and the headless balance model already cover deterministic rules; this pass is for touch clarity, portrait readability, pacing and the feeling of caring for one persistent farm.

Start from **Reset farm**, play for at least two complete Spring Weeks, and reload the page at least once during each Week. Do not optimize only for coins: try one mixed farm and deliberately keep at least one cared-for crop across a day or Week boundary.

- [ ] The opening shows exactly three Land: one Empty Plot and two Wild Soil. No action, harvest, reload or Week transition creates a fourth Land.
- [ ] Farmer → General Store opens five physical Seed cards. Cost, first harvest, yield and after-harvest behavior are readable without leaving the tray.
- [ ] Carrot feels fast and disposable; Green Bean feels like a persistent Land commitment; Potato's required Hoe and `2 + 2` digs are obvious; Cauliflower feels slow but worth protecting; Radish creates a real baby-now versus full-later choice.
- [ ] With only three Land, at least one Seed purchase is postponed because another crop feels more important—not because the UI is confusing.
- [ ] Every crop can be sown both water-first and seed-first. Rain on Spring Day 6 is legible and useful; it does not silently push Baby Radishes past their choice.
- [ ] Sickle removal is understandable for growing/mature crops, yields nothing, and feels like an intentional way to reclaim Land rather than a trap.
- [ ] Dragging a growing crop onto free-handed Farmer and the equivalent tap path both Tend it. The 1 AP cost, Tended card transform and valid target are visible before committing.
- [ ] A Tended harvest becomes a clearly different Choice Produce card. Ordinary and Choice stacks remain separate, Hand ordering stays readable, and shipping them at the same coin rate does not make care feel pointless because the memory persists.
- [ ] Green Bean asks for care again after regrowth; Potato keeps care through both digs; removing a tended crop clears its pending Quality without producing anything.
- [ ] Reloading preserves crop progress, Baby Radish choice, Potato Mounds, Tended state, Hand quantities, queued shipments, Area, AP, Week and farm memories. Reloading never changes yield or Quality.
- [ ] Tap and drag continue to resolve through the same engine for Store, Tools, sowing, watering, harvesting, tending, shipping and Landmark travel. Failed actions spend no AP.
- [ ] Hold on both board and Hand cards opens one enlarged card with its description below. It is discoverable without causing accidental play or drag.
- [ ] Five Seed/Produce families, three Tools and two Landmarks remain scannable in the portrait Hand. The board still leaves enough room to arrange Farmer and three Land cards.
- [ ] Ending the day in Town wakes Farmer and the carried card at Home Farm. Free equip, purchase and shipping actions remain understandable beside paid work.
- [ ] The Weekly Journal reads like a memory of care: first harvest, crop discoveries, first Choice crops and farm milestones stand out more than totals. Crops still living on the farm feel continuous into the next Week.
- [ ] After two Weeks, 8 AP feels like a planning constraint rather than repetitive interruption. Note any day dominated by refill/travel chores and which crop caused it.
- [ ] Record the first moment that felt affectionate or persistent, the first confusing card state, any crop that was never tempting, and any strategy that seemed obviously dominant.

Stop only after the second **Continue farm** begins and confirm the same Land identities, living crops, supplies and memory book are still present.
