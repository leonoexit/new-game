# Prototype 1 — Card Grammar + Person Presence hands-on checklist

This is the single manual checklist for the milestone. Play at least two complete Spring Weeks. Reload once during each Week. Do not rebalance 8 AP from automated evidence alone; record the felt tradeoff here first.

## Card scan

- [ ] World cards read consistently as family/status → art → title → progress or quantity.
- [ ] Family colors help classification without coloring the whole card.
- [ ] Choice, Tended and relationship state do not compete with quantity or progress.
- [ ] Cyan selection and gold valid/drop targeting remain unmistakable.
- [ ] Hand, Store and Inspection feel like the same card language.
- [ ] The General Store no longer produces unusually tall cards.
- [ ] Hold reveals detail; normal board cards stay quiet enough to scan.

## Week 1

- [ ] Meet Mira in Town on day 1.
- [ ] See Bram at the Farm and Nell in Town on day 2.
- [ ] Use both tap and drag to resolve Person moments.
- [ ] Spend Time with free hands and confirm exactly 1 AP is spent.
- [ ] Share ordinary Produce and confirm exactly 1 AP and one Produce are spent.
- [ ] Confirm a Tool and a Seed cannot be shared.
- [ ] Confirm the same Person refuses a second moment that day without mutation.
- [ ] Hold each Person and verify identity, rhythm, interests and Memory are legible.
- [ ] Reload during the Week and confirm schedule, layout and Memory survive.
- [ ] Finish day 7 and confirm Person highlights appear in the Weekly Journal.

## Week 2

- [ ] Continue Farm and confirm relationships persist while day-1 presence returns.
- [ ] Reload again during the Week.
- [ ] Meet all three residents across the two-Week run.
- [ ] Create at least two different memories with one Person and see `Familiar`.
- [ ] Try repeating an existing signature and confirm no AP or Produce is lost.
- [ ] Create four memories across at least two dates with at least one Share and see `Close`.
- [ ] Share a preferred crop or Choice Produce and confirm it changes memory/Journal wording only.
- [ ] Judge whether Person moments create a meaningful farming-versus-life AP choice at 8 AP.

## Report back

Record confusing cards, missed schedules, accidental interactions, AP pressure and whether the three residents feel present without dialogue. Separate UI readability problems from balance problems.
