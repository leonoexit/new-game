## Prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept. A character-focused native pixel-art event vignette for a cozy farming life game. Mira quietly offers a tired farmer a warm cup of tea outside her modest café after rain. Original high-key chibi characters about two-and-a-half heads tall, expressive readable body language, strong hair and clothing silhouettes, colored outlines, clean three-step shading, deliberate hard-edged pixel clusters. Mira has a compact silhouette, short dark-chestnut bob, honey-amber apron, tiny berry-red hair tie; one steaming tea cup. Character detail dominates a simple café doorway and amber window. Fresh light colors with compact cool-teal shadows. Horizontal 3:2 composition, camera chosen naturally for the interaction. ## Review
```

### Keep

- The interaction reads immediately and the characters carry the scene.
- Chibi proportions, silhouettes, and facial expressions are clear at phone size.
- Bright outdoor blues and warm café light work well together.

### Change

- Still leans toward anime rendered through a pixel filter rather than disciplined low-resolution pixel art.
- Background is far more detailed than necessary.
- Character proportions and large glossy eyes may be too cute for the intended emotional range.

## Decision

Keep as a comparison study only. Do not promote to canonical art direction.
